Shawn Sim's wedding, pre-wedding and solemnisation photography site (he/him, Singapore). Replaces
https://shawnyscaptures.myportfolio.com/. Sibling of the design portfolio at
`/Users/shawnys/Portfolio/design-portfolio`; it shares that site's frames and interactions, not its colours.

- `brand/BRAND.md`, `brand/SKILL.md`: the Shawnys Captures brand (v2: neutral frames on the website,
  photos carry the colour). Copied on 2026-11-11 from `/Users/shawnys/Portfolio/Brand/shawnys-captures/`,
  which stays the source of truth; change both together.
- `docs/SITE-AUDIT.md`: crawl of the old site (IA, prices, FAQ, form fields, problems, proposed IA).
- `data/site.json`: albums (cover, featured, photos, chapters, old URL for redirects), pricing, contact.
  Photo URLs are stand-ins on the old site's CDN until the photos are re-exported to Cloudinary;
  `null` means still to write or confirm.
- `public/assets/`: logo in its three approved inks, the three rays.
- `drafts/v2/`: **draft v0.3 (2026-11-11)**, the current one. One page on hash routes: `#/` opens on a page
  of a photobook, one per landing album, turning to the next on a timer; scrolling grows the page's main
  photograph to full screen. Per album in `site.json`: `layout` (`row`, `inset` or `split`), `trio` (left,
  main, right photographs), and for `inset` a `ground` photo and `insetAt` positions (`landing.js` + `landing.css`),
  then a tab bar that sticks to the top (Weddings, Family, Events, Others, Rates, FAQ: `#/weddings` ... `#/faq`),
  a small about section whose copy and picture follow the open tab (`about` in `site.json`), and the tab's
  content (a wall of album boxes, the rates, or the FAQ). `#/album/<slug>` and `#/enquire` are separate views
  under the same bar. `index.html` + `site.js` + `site.css` hold all of it; `base.css` holds the tokens
  (bright neutral white, Wittgenstein, the design portfolio's spacing and radii). `concepts/a|b|c.html` are the
  earlier v0.2 landing studies with their own `concepts/base.css`. Serve the folder root (launch config
  `photo-draft`, port 4341) and open `/drafts/v2/index.html`.
- `drafts/home.html`: draft v0.1 (neutral frames, album panel). Superseded; kept for reference.
- `public/textures/`: paper grain, the long-exposure landscape prints (`print-*.webp`, the grounds of boxes with
  no photograph) and the v0.2 light leaks, generated by `python3 scripts/make-textures.py`

- `PROJECT.md`: status, decisions, acceptance criteria and open questions.

## Adding and removing albums

`data/site.json` is the only place albums live; every page reads it.

- `node scripts/albums.mjs` opens the album manager at http://localhost:4342: add an album (title, category,
  type, venue, a photo folder such as `public/photos/<slug>` or a list of photo addresses), remove one,
  reorder, and choose which show full-screen on the landing.
- The same from the command line: `node scripts/albums.mjs list | add | remove <slug> | landing <slug> on|off |
  move <slug> <position>` (the options are at the top of the script).
- Each change backs up the previous file to `data/site.backup.json`.
- Categories are `weddings`, `family`, `events`, `others` (`categories` in `site.json`); `type` is the finer
  label shown on the album (Wedding, Pre-wedding, Solemnisation, Maternity, ...).
