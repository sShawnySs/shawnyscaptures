# Shawnys Captures brand

The full guidelines are in `BRAND.md`, next to this file. Read it before designing anything new. This page is the working checklist.

## The idea
**Waiting for good light.** The mood is warm evening: golden hour, textured and hand-made. It's the warm sibling of the cool Shawn Sim Designs.

## Palette (use only these)
- Captures (warm): Golden Hour `#F26B2E` (signature), Honey `#E0A13A`, Terracotta `#B0452A` (small accent text on light), Rosehip `#7E2A1C` (deep warm ground).
- Bridge (shared): Linen `#FFFAEB`, Oat `#EFE6CC`, Umber `#6B5A4C`, Darkroom `#3C291A`.
- **Website (v2, 2026-11-11): neutral frames.** Grounds are Paper `#FAFAF7` frames on a Seam `#2B2B28`, and Viewer `#141413` behind full-screen photos; text Ink `#1C1C1A` / Ink Quiet `#5E5E59` (Quiet Dark `#A3A39C` on Viewer). Warm colours are marks only: Terracotta for links on Paper, Golden Hour for rays and hover fills (never text on Paper), Honey for text accents on Viewer. One optional accent per album, sampled from its cover, at ≥4.5:1. Frames, radii and motion follow the design brand. No leaf shadow or grain on the site.
- **Print and social:** the default ground is **Darkroom**, with Rosehip for cards and Linen for text-heavy pieces. Never use pure white or pure black.
- **Never** use Pine `#36594F`, Harbour `#4A6B78`, Eucalyptus `#8FA89B` or Sea Glass `#DCE8E4`. They belong to the design brand.

## Contrast guardrails
- Golden Hour and Honey take Darkroom text.
- Rosehip and Darkroom take Linen, Oat or Honey text.
- On Linen, small accent text is Terracotta. Golden Hour appears only at 44 px+.

## Type
- Display: **Averia Serif Libre** Regular 400, with *italic* 400 for the emphasised phrase. Never bold. (Fraunces is retired.)
- Body/UI/captions: **Ringside Regular** (Semibold for captions). If the licensed files aren't available, use **Libre Franklin** and say it's a stand-in.
- Script: only Shawn's hand-lettering, one word at a time. In mockups, La Belle Aurore is a stand-in; label it as one.
- Averia Serif Libre belongs to this brand only; the design brand no longer uses it.

## Logo
- Use the files in `assets/`: `shawnys-logo-linen.png` (on Darkroom or Rosehip), `shawnys-logo-darkroom.png` (on Linen or Golden Hour), `shawnys-logo-golden-hour.png` (on Darkroom or Rosehip).
- Never redraw, retype, recolour outside these, stretch or add effects. Clear space is the height of the rays. Minimum width is 120 px.

## Texture
- Leaf-shadow overlay (blurred rotated ellipses, 10–25%), film grain on Darkroom and Rosehip, and the three rays in Golden Hour as bullets, loaders and favicon.
- Card radius 16–24 px; pills for tags and nav. No drop shadows, gradient washes or emoji.

## Voice
Warm, observant, unhurried. Write in first person with plain words and specific details. No exclamation marks, hype ("magical", "cinematic experiences") or scarcity pressure.

## Before handing work back
1. Only warm, bridge and (on the website) web-neutral colours are used, with no cool design accents; nothing tints or frames a photo in colour.
2. Text contrast is ≥4.5:1 (≥3:1 at 24 px+).
3. Averia Serif Libre is used for display and Ringside (or a labelled stand-in) for everything else.
4. The logo is an approved ink on an approved ground, with clear space.
5. Copy reads warm and specific, with no exclamation marks.
6. Placeholders such as `[email]` are listed for the user, never invented.
