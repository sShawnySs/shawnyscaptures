# Shawnys Captures — Brand Guidelines

Photography by Shawn Sim. The **warm, evening**, crafted half of the Shawnys family.
Sister brand: **Shawn Sim Designs** (product design, cool daylight). The two share only the *bridge* (see §3).

Deck (both brands, photography is Part 2): https://claude.ai/artifact/QozcVvahvNSGAoYYi49j6h

**v2, the website (Shawn, 2026-11-11): neutral frames, photos are the colour.** The site uses Shawn Sim
Designs' frame system and interactions (paper frames a hairline apart on a seam, 16 px corners, blur-in
entrance, custom cursor, the panel), but its grounds are neutral so they never tint a photo. The warmth
lives in the photos, Averia Serif and a few small warm marks. The warm grounds (Darkroom, Rosehip, Linen,
Oat) stay for print, social and stationery. Changes are marked **v2**. The current site's content and IA
are in `SITE-AUDIT.md`.

## 1. Idea

**Waiting for good light.**

A photograph works when the light finally makes a moment legible: a laugh, a glance, sun through leaves. Shawnys Captures waits for that moment instead of forcing it.

- **Mood:** evening, golden hour, warm, textured, hand-made.
- **Personality:** crafted / editorial, calm / thoughtful.
- **Services:** Weddings · Pre-weddings · Portraits *(confirm)*. Signature offer: Wedding Day + Pre-Wedding bundle.

## 2. Logo

- The existing hand-lettered **Shawnys Captures** mark (`Photography Branding/v6.svg`). Never redraw, retype or re-letter it.
- **v2 website:** Darkroom ink on Paper (13.2:1); Linen ink over a photo only on a calm area or a Viewer scrim.
- Approved inks: **Linen** on Darkroom or Rosehip (primary), **Darkroom** on Linen (daylight), **Darkroom** on Golden Hour (statement), and **Golden Hour** on Darkroom or Rosehip.
- Recoloured PNGs: `assets/shawnys-logo-linen.png`, `assets/shawnys-logo-darkroom.png`, `assets/shawnys-logo-golden-hour.png`.
- Clear space = the height of the three rays. Minimum 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
- Don't stretch, outline, add shadows or glows, or use any ink outside the approved ones. On photos, place it on a calm area or over a Darkroom scrim at 40–60%.

**Rules (v2, website)**
- Grounds are Paper, Seam and Viewer only. No warm grounds, washes or gradients behind photos.
- Warmth comes from: the photos; Averia Serif display type; **Terracotta** for links and small accent text
  on Paper (5.4:1); **Golden Hour** only on the three rays, hover and selection fills, and text on Viewer
  (6.1:1), never as text on Paper (2.9:1); **Honey** for text accents on Viewer (8.2:1).
- **Album accent:** each album may take one accent sampled from its cover photo, used the way a design case
  study uses its theme (the album's opener label, its chapter numbers, link underlines). It must reach
  4.5:1 against its ground; if the sampled colour doesn't, darken it until it does (on Paper) or lighten it
  (on Viewer).
- Photos are never cropped by a coloured frame, overlaid with tint or given a filter. Thumbnail crops use
  the album's `focus` (see `SITE-AUDIT.md`).
- Never use Pine, Harbour, Eucalyptus or Sea Glass here. Those belong to the design brand. Moss is the
  design brand's signature; don't use it here either.

**Rules (print, social, stationery)**
- Golden Hour on Linen is too faint for small text. Use it for fills and 44 px+ type; small accent text uses Terracotta.
- Default grounds: Darkroom and Rosehip, alternating with Linen for text-heavy pieces.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | **Averia Serif Libre** (Google Fonts; moved here from the design brand, 2026-10-01) | Regular 400 for headlines; *italic* 400 carries the emphasised phrase. Soft, slightly blurred and hand-made, so it suits warm evening light. Never bold |
| Body / UI / captions | **Ringside** (Hoefler&Co, licensed; bridge) | Libre Franklin is the stand-in until the licensed files are installed. Captions are Semibold caps with +0.12em tracking |
| Signature word | **Your hand-lettering** (bridge) | Same pen as the logo, one word at a time. La Belle Aurore is only a mockup stand-in |

Web scale: 72 / 44 / 28 / 18 / 14 px. Display line-height 1.05 with −1 to −2 px tracking at 44 px and up, body 1.55. Headline pattern: plain words, then an *italic* phrase ("Waiting for *good light.*"). Fraunces is retired.

## 5. Texture & motifs

- **Leaf shadow:** blurred, rotated ellipses at 10–25% opacity. Use Rosehip or Darkroom on light and Golden Hour grounds, and Golden Hour or Honey on dark grounds. It drifts slowly on scroll. Use it on heroes and colour fields, never over faces.
- **Film grain:** fractal noise at 12–35% on Darkroom and Rosehip grounds only.
- **Three rays (bridge):** taken from the logo, for bullets, loaders, favicon and the "new" marker. Here they're in Golden Hour.
- Corners: 16–24 px radius. Pills for tags and nav.
- **v2 website:** leaf shadow and film grain are print and social only; on the site they'd sit next to
  photos. The rays stay (loader, favicon, "new" marker). Radii follow the design brand's frame rules:
  16 px frames, 8 px inside frames, 999 pills, nested corners concentric.

## 6. Imagery

- Natural, directional light. Never flat on-camera flash.
- Foliage, shadow and texture in the frame.
- Candid over posed.
- Warm grade: lift blacks toward Darkroom and push highlights toward Honey.
- Black and white is welcome for weddings.

## 7. Voice

**Warm, observant, unhurried.** First person ("I"), plain words, specific details. No exclamation marks, no scarcity pressure.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Let's find your good light." | "Capturing your magical moments forever!!!" |
| "Tell me about the two of you first." | "Limited slots, book now!" |
| "Golden hour starts around [time]. Let's meet 30 minutes before." | "Cinematic storytelling experiences." |
